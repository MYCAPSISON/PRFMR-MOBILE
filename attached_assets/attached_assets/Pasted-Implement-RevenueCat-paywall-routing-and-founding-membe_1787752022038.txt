Implement RevenueCat paywall routing and founding-member purchase handling in my Expo/React Native PRFMR mobile app.

Existing setup:

RevenueCat entitlement:
- `PRFMR Pro`

RevenueCat offerings:
- `default`
- `founding`

RevenueCat packages in BOTH offerings:
- `$rc_monthly`
- `$rc_annual`

iOS products:
- Monthly: `prfmr_pro_monthly`
- Annual: `prfmr_pro_annual`

Android products/base plans:
- Monthly: `prfmr_monthly:monthly`
- Annual: `prfmr_yearly:yearly`

Google Play founding offers:
- Monthly offer ID: `founding-monthly`
- Annual offer ID: `founding-yearly`

The Google founding offers are already configured as developer-determined offers:
- monthly = 25% off for 12 monthly billing periods, then normal price
- annual = 30% off for the first annual period, then normal price

Apple founding discounts use Offer Codes attached to the NORMAL iOS products:
- `prfmr_pro_monthly`
- `prfmr_pro_annual`

The Apple monthly founding Offer Code offer gives 25% off for 12 months.
The Apple annual founding Offer Code offer gives 30% off for the first year.
Production one-time Apple codes cannot be generated until App Review approval, so Sandbox Offer Codes will be used during testing.

Database:

- column `is_founding` is boolean
- `true` means the account is a Founding Fighter
- do NOT use email to determine founding eligibility
- use the authenticated user's stable user ID for RevenueCat customer identity
